About our school

The Engineering School in Navoi is part of the Agency for Specialized Educational Institutions and focuses on engineering education. Students connect their knowledge of mathematics and natural sciences with practical engineering preparation.

7–10 Admission grades in 2026/2027

THE SCHOOL DAY

How is the school day organised?

The school day starts at 8:30 a.m. The number of regular lessons depends on the grade, with no more than seven in a day. After regular lessons, all students attend a compulsory one-hour extra class in STEM subjects. There are no lessons on Saturdays.

Grade 7 Students have seven regular lessons on two days each week. The number of lessons on other days follows the class timetable.
Grade 10 Grade 10 students have seven regular lessons every school day.
STEM session After regular lessons, students attend a compulsory one-hour extra session focused on STEM subjects.

FIVE-YEAR EDUCATION PROGRAMME

From knowledge to engineering

The programme is built step by step: students first develop a strong STEM foundation, then become familiar with engineering subjects and university-level learning.

Years 1–3

A foundation in core subjects

Particular attention is given to physics, mathematics and foreign languages. These subjects provide the foundation for understanding engineering challenges and preparing for the next stage.

Years 4–5

Preparation for engineering

Students deepen their preparation in engineering subjects. Grades 10–11 also include classes integrated with first-year subjects from a related university programme.

01

STEM-based learning

The five-year programme begins with physics, mathematics and foreign languages, followed by introductory preparation in engineering subjects.

02

University partnership

Navoi State University of Mining and Technologies is the school’s partner in delivering its engineering education programme.

03

Practical experience

In Grade 11, students complete a two-month work placement at an enterprise related to their chosen engineering field.

The next step in higher education

Graduates who meet the academic requirements and score at least 1300 on the SAT, or hold a B2 certificate in mathematics, physics or chemistry, may continue from the second year of a related university engineering programme with an additional state grant.

OUR TEACHERS

Knowledge and skills are put to the test

Teachers also take knowledge assessments similar to the exams taken by students. Candidates go through a rigorous selection process across the region and demonstrate their knowledge and professional preparation. Visit the Exams page to learn more about the selection stages and exams.
