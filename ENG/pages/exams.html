ADMISSIONS AND ENTRANCE EXAM

Admission to Grade 7 at the Engineering School

Admission to Grade 7 at Navoi Engineering School is competitive and is open to pupils who have completed Grade 6. This guide explains the test format, the number of questions, and the main steps in the admission process.

Grade 7The grade pupils enter
48 placesQuota for the 2026/2027 school year
45 questionsMathematics and English
90 minutesTime allowed for the exam

WHO CAN APPLY?

The next step after Grade 6

The entrance selection is for pupils completing Grade 6. During the summer admission period, applicants submit their documents and sit the entrance exam. Passing the selection gives pupils the opportunity to continue into Grade 7 and study in an engineering-focused school.

For the 2026/2027 school year, Navoi City Engineering School has an approved Grade 7 quota of 48 places. Places and application dates are set for each school year, so check the latest official announcement before applying.

After the exam, applicants are ranked by their total scores. Within the approved quota, the 48 applicants with the highest results are selected for admission.

TEST FORMAT

45 questions across two subjects

The exam assesses mathematics and English.

01

Mathematics

30 questions

Applicants need to demonstrate problem-solving, logical reasoning, and calculation skills.

02

English

15 questions

English is assessed separately and contributes to the overall selection result.

Total: 45 test questionsApplicants are given 90 minutes to complete the exam.

ON EXAM DAY

Venue and preparation

The exam is held at Navoiy Test Markazi. The exact date, start time, admission procedure, and required documents are provided in the admission announcement or exam permit. Check those details before travelling and plan to arrive at the centre early.

Managing the 90-minute time limit is part of preparation. Read each question carefully, answer the ones you know first, and leave time at the end to review your work. Follow the exam rules and bring only permitted items.

Prepare for both subjects: mathematics has more questions, but English also affects the final selection result. Regular practice under timed conditions and reviewing mistakes can help applicants feel more confident on exam day.

RESULTS AND APPLICATIONS

Follow official announcements

Under the official admission rules, applications are submitted through the designated online portals. Exam results are published within 20 days of the exam. Applicants who disagree with a result may submit an appeal through the established procedure within three working days after results are published.

Application dates, required documents, quotas, and exam instructions can change each school year. Compare the information on this page with the latest official notices.
