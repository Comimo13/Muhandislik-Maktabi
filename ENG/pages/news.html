INTELLECTUAL CHALLENGE

🧠 “ZAKOVAT” — a challenge of knowledge and thinking

The Engineering School held a “Zakovat” intellectual quiz for its students. During the event, students formed teams and answered engaging logic questions from a range of subjects.

Teams discussed the questions together, drawing on their knowledge and quick thinking. The game encouraged logical reasoning, quick decision-making, collaboration, and explaining the reasoning behind an answer. It took place in a friendly and lively atmosphere. 🎓
